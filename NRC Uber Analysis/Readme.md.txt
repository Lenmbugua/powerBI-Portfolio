🚖 Uber Analysis – NCR Ride Bookings
📝 Introduction

This project analyzes ride-hailing bookings in the NCR region using Power BI to uncover patterns in booking volume, cancellations, revenue, vehicle type performance, and payment methods. The goal is to transform raw ride data into actionable insights for improving operations, customer satisfaction, and revenue strategies.

📄 Abstract

The dataset contains 150K+ ride bookings with details on booking status, cancellations (driver vs customer), payment methods, booking value, vehicle type, and ride counts.

This project applies:

Data Cleaning & Transformation (Power Query)

Data Modeling (DAX measures for KPIs)

Interactive Visualizations (Power BI dashboards)

The result is a dashboard that helps stakeholders track performance and identify business opportunities.

📊 Overview

Dataset Size: 150,000+ rows

Key Columns:

Booking ID

Booking Status (Completed, Cancelled by Driver, Cancelled by Customer, No Driver Found, Incomplete)

Vehicle Type (Auto, Go Mini, Go Sedan, Bike, etc.)

Payment Method (UPI, Cash, Wallet, Cards)

Booking Value (₹)

Tools Used:

Power BI (visualization & dashboarding)

DAX (measures & KPIs)

Power Query (data cleaning & transformation)

🎯 Goals

Track total rides, cancellations, and completion rates.

Compare performance across vehicle types.

Identify revenue drivers (vehicle mix, payment method).

Highlight operational challenges (driver vs customer cancellations).

Provide stakeholders with an interactive dashboard for decision-making.

🌍 Domain Document

Ride-hailing is a core part of urban mobility. The industry faces challenges like:

Cancellations (supply-demand mismatch, driver reliability, customer indecision).

Payment preferences (digital adoption vs cash).

Fleet management (balancing affordable vs premium vehicles).

Revenue optimization (distance, pricing, and ride mix).

Analyzing NCR ride data allows stakeholders to:

Improve driver allocation

Optimize fleet mix

Encourage digital payments

Enhance service reliability

🔎 Insights & Findings

Total Rides: ~149K rides were booked, with a steady growth trend.

Cancellations: ~27K driver cancellations vs ~11K customer cancellations → driver-side issues dominate.

Booking Status: ~93K completed rides (~62% completion rate), but cancellations + no-driver cases remain significant.

Vehicle Mix: Auto, Go Mini, and Go Sedan account for the majority of rides; premium vehicles contribute fewer trips but higher value.

Revenue: UPI and Cash lead in revenue share, showing customer preference for fast digital and cash transactions.

Customer Behavior: Most trips are short and affordable; longer-distance premium trips are less frequent but valuable.

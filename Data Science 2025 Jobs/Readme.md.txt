Data Science Jobs Dashboard
Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of data science job postings, including salaries, skills, employers, and career progression. The goal is to help job seekers, employers, and educators understand current trends in the data science job market.

The dataset used is cleaned_datascience_data.csv, which contains job postings with details such as job title, salary, location, skills, company, and seniority.

Insights

Salaries vary significantly by city and job type.

Remote jobs often pay more than on-site roles in certain cities.

Python, SQL, and Machine Learning remain the top in-demand skills.

Career progression opportunities are most concentrated at mid-level roles.

A few companies dominate job postings, but smaller employers also compete on salary.

Recommendations

Job Seekers: Focus on learning top 5 skills, compare remote vs. on-site pay, and target top employers.

Employers: Benchmark salaries by city and highlight career progression opportunities.

Educators: Build training programs around hottest and emerging skills.

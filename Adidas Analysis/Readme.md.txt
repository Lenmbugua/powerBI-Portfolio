Adidas US Sales Analysis
📌 Overview

This project analyzes Adidas US sales data (2020–2021) to uncover sales performance trends across regions, products, and retailers. The insights are visualized in a Power BI dashboard, helping identify revenue drivers, market opportunities, and consumer preferences.

🎯 Business Requirements

Through this Power BI-driven analysis, Adidas aims to:

Enhance understanding of sales dynamics and performance drivers.

Identify geographical areas with high and low sales potential.

Provide insights into product performance, aiding inventory and marketing decisions.

Inform pricing and margin strategies for improved profitability.

Deliver actionable recommendations for optimizing sales and profit across various dimensions.

🛠 Problem Statement & KPIs

KPI	                   	Description
Total Sales Analysis	Understand overall sales performance of Adidas over time.

Profitability Analysis	Evaluate total profit generated across different dimensions.

Sales Volume Analysis	Examine total units sold to gain insights into product demand.

Pricing Strategy		Determine the average price per unit to assess the pricing approach.

Margin Analysis			Evaluate average margin to understand the overall profitability of sales.

By answering these, the company can improve decision-making and resource allocation.

🎯 Objectives of the Analysis

Track monthly sales trends to identify seasonal peaks.

Compare sales performance by region and state.

Evaluate retailer contributions to total revenue.

Measure profitability by product category.

Provide actionable insights for business strategy.

📊 Dashboard & Methodology

The analysis was conducted in Power BI using:

Data Cleaning & Modeling: Removing duplicates, creating calculated fields.

Visualizations: Line charts (monthly trend), bar charts (region & product sales), pie charts (retailer distribution), KPIs (profit, units sold).

📊 Chart / Visual Requirements
Chart / Visual	Purpose

Total Sales by Month (Area Chart)	Visualize monthly distribution of total sales to identify peak periods.

Total Sales by State (Filled Map)	Geographically represent total sales across different states.

Total Sales by Region (Donut Chart)	Represent the contribution of different regions to total sales.

Total Sales by Product (Bar Chart)	Analyze sales distribution among various Adidas products.

Total Sales by Retailer (Bar Chart)	Visualize the contribution of different retailers to total sales.

🔍 Key Insights

Sales Performance by Time

Peak month: March ($95M), followed by December ($92M).

Lowest sales: April ($57M), showing possible seasonal demand dips.

Regional Sales Distribution

West ($270M, 30%) is the top-performing region.

Northeast & Southeast contribute significantly (~39%).

Midwest and South have relatively lower shares.

Top Retailers

West Gear ($243M) and Foot Locker ($220M) dominate retailer sales.

Walmart and Amazon underperform compared to specialized sports retailers.

Product Category Insights

Men’s Street Footwear ($209M) is the best-selling category.

Women’s products also perform strongly, but lag behind men’s in revenue.

Athletic footwear and apparel show steady contributions.

Profitability

High operating margin of 42% reflects strong pricing power and demand.

Opportunity to scale underperforming retailers (Walmart & Amazon) for growth.

✅ Conclusion

The analysis reveals that Adidas US sales are strongest in the West region, driven by men’s footwear and partnerships with specialty retailers. Seasonal spikes in March and December suggest promotional or holiday-driven demand. To grow further, Adidas can boost performance in underrepresented regions, strengthen e-commerce presence, and expand women’s product marketing.
